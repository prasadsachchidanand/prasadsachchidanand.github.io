Quiz Information & Setup

Our quiz features a diverse collection of mathematical questions covering topics such as Real Analysis, Algebra, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Metric Space, Numerical Methods, and more. Customize your experience by selecting a specific topic (or choose "All" for a mixed set) and set your preferred quiz duration between 1 minute and 120 minutes.

  • Topics: Questions come from various fields including Real Analysis, Algebra, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Metric Space, and Numerical Methods. Use the topic selector to focus on your area of interest or select "All" for a broad mix.
  • Number of Questions: The quiz consists of 10 randomly selected questions from our extensive question bank.
  • Marking Scheme:
    • +1 point for each correct answer
    • -1/3 point for each incorrect answer (if attempted)
    • 0 points for unattempted questions
  • Time Limit: Customize your quiz duration anywhere from 1 minute to 120 minutes.
  • Feedback: Once submitted, your quiz will be graded immediately. Correct answers will be highlighted.
  • Setup: Please provide your name, email, quiz duration, and select your preferred topic(s) on the setup page before starting.
