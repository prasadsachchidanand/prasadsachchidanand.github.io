Linear Algebra Calculator

Row reduction, rank, null space, systems, inverse, determinant, eigenvalues, eigenvectors and diagonalization, with every step. All arithmetic is exact: fractions stay fractions, and eigenvalues such as \(1 \pm \sqrt{2}\) or \(1 \pm i\) are kept exact.

One row per line; separate the entries by spaces or commas. Fractions (3/4), decimals (0.5) and negative numbers are fine. For a system \(Ax = b\), put | before the last column. Up to 8 × 8 (eigenvalues up to 6 × 6).

Made for the Linear Algebra course (MTH100) at IIIT Delhi; the notation follows Lay, Linear Algebra and Its Applications. Try the self-assessment questions too.
